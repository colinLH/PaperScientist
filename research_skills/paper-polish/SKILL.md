# Paper polish

Read enough context to preserve the scientific argument. Improve structure before sentence-level edits. Each paragraph should make one claim, explain the mechanism or evidence, and connect to the next step. Prefer concrete descriptions over promotional adjectives.

At the first complete draft, load manuscript-quality and writing-style:anti-defensive-writing. Save a review_manuscript baseline before editing, then a revised report afterward. Make section-specific revisions, not just a generic checklist. Preserve the evidence and real limitations while removing formulaic language, repeated hedges and unsupported flourishes. Check numeric/citation changes, validate, compile and inspect the final PDF. Style changes do not promise to evade detectors or establish scientific merit.

Use consistent terminology for modules, variables, baselines and datasets. Make motivation and challenges specific. State contributions as verifiable changes and outcomes. Calibrate “demonstrate”, “suggest”, “may” and “hypothesize” to the evidence. Do not upgrade correlation to causation or preliminary findings to general claims.

Preserve LaTeX commands, labels, citations, equations and measured values. Check abstract/introduction/conclusion consistency with actual results. Report substantive scientific ambiguities rather than silently choosing an interpretation.

Use write_file with intentional overwrite after reading the source. Recompile after edits where possible. For an independent review, delegate the draft and claim ledger to a reviewer and resolve actionable findings. Return changed files and unresolved issues.
