# Work gallery sources

Verified on 2026-10-29 (Asia/Shanghai). The gallery links to authors’ official project pages and streams their own published demo media. No third-party site scripts or analytics are embedded. External media remain subject to their authors’ terms and availability.

The 36 projects cover neural rendering, 3D/4D generation, image/video editing and robotics. Venue labels are used where confirmed on the project page; otherwise the footer describes the research area.

Desktop: staggered rails with four substantial previews and up to five including partial edge entrants. Compact screens: two. At most two video sources and five poster sources are attached; offscreen sources are released. The shared animation loop sleeps when the gallery is stationary. Wheel gestures still move one third of the viewport.

## Verification

- All 36 project URLs and 36 videos returned HTTP 200/206; all 34 remote posters returned image responses. Two local poster frames are served by the application. Requests read a bounded initial range, not full media files.
- Playback is checked separately in the browser. HTTP availability alone does not establish browser decoding support, autoplay permission or future availability. Failed previews retain the original-site link and an explicit fallback.
